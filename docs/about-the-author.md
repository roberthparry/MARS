# About the Author

Robert H Parry (Rob) is the sole creator and maintainer of the MARS library.

Born in Salford, Rob spent his first years of schooling in Waterlooville,
followed by four years in Zambia. His interest in mathematics began at the age
of twelve, driven by a fascination with where mathematical formulae came from
and why they worked. He learnt celestial navigation at fourteen and subsequently
learnt programming using programmable calculators, writing navigation programs
intended for use by yachtsmen.

After completing his A levels in North Wales, Rob studied physics and
applied mathematics at the University of the Witwatersrand, Johannesburg, where
he received the Herbert Le May Book Prize and a monetary scholarship prize
conditional on his continuing his studies at Cambridge, which he chose not to
do. He specialised in general relativity and cosmology, including master's-level
courses in both subjects. His varied early work included working as a quality
control technician at South African Breweries and undertaking some research at
the National Institute for Nuclear Physics, South Africa.

Rob subsequently joined the CSIR to fulfil the obligations of a university
bursary. He worked first in materials research as a mathematical modeller and
later in advanced computing, decision support and management information
systems. During this period, he also worked as an independent music DJ at night
and at weekends. His work at the CSIR developed his interest in scientific
computing and its application to practical problems.

In 1991, Rob moved to Cape Town and entered the commercial software industry.
After an unpromising first appointment, he joined a company where he remained
for twenty-one years. He later returned to Britain. As changes in the working
environment left less scope for the creative aspects of programming he most
valued, his parallel and equally long-standing fascination with finding meaning
in data and using it to guide decisions offered a natural change of direction.
He became a reporting and data analyst. Throughout this transition, he continued
to write C and explore scientific computing as a personal interest.

Outside his professional work, Rob has undertaken cycle tours, enjoyed snorkel
diving and won cups at school for .22-calibre rifle shooting. He has also done
some yachting, though his enthusiasm for celestial navigation has outlasted his
tolerance for cold, wet days at sea beneath a potentially lethal boom. Although
he no longer enjoys travelling as much as he once did, he has greatly enjoyed
recent visits to Greece and the Azores. His Dutch family
connections have also taken him to the Netherlands since childhood,
particularly Amsterdam, Limmen, Castricum and Alkmaar. Earlier travels, many
years ago, also took him to Swaziland, Bahrain, Botswana, Nigeria and Australia.

MARS brings together his interests in navigation and computer programming with
a deeper interest in how we perceive the world around us and in the mathematics
that governs its workings.
