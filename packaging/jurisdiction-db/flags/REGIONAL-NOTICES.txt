de-sat.svg: Saterland municipal flag, original design Bernd Stolle; SVG by
Ætoms, 25 February 2021. Unchanged SVG. Wikimedia Commons marks the official
flag public domain (PD-Flag-Germany); its structured record also records
CC BY-SA 4.0. Retain attribution and that licence for any adaptations.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:DEU_Saterland_Flag.svg
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

de-sor.svg: original MARS geometric SVG encoding of the Sorbian blue/red/white
horizontal tricolour, under the repository MIT Licence.
Design reference: Landkreis Bautzen, "Das Volk der Sorben".
https://www.landkreis-bautzen.de/sorben.php

fi-sap.svg: Sámi flag, original design Astrid Båhl (1986); SVG by Jeltz,
with later simplification by Wikimedia Commons contributors. Public-domain
dedication by Jeltz, with an unrestricted fallback grant. Unchanged SVG.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sami_flag.svg

fr-bre.svg: Gwenn ha du, original design Morvan Marchal; SVG from OpenClipart,
Gryffindor and Alkari (25 August 2012), public domain. Unchanged SVG.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Brittany_(Gwenn_ha_du).svg

fr-cor.svg: Corsican flag, Patricia.fidi/OpenClipart, public-domain dedication
(CC0). The unused external SVG DOCTYPE was removed; artwork is unchanged.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Corsica.svg
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

it-88.svg: Sardinian flag, icnussa; derivative Xander89. Unchanged SVG,
distributed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported.
Credit the authors, retain this licence link, and distribute adaptations under
the same or a compatible licence. No endorsement is implied.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Sardinia.svg
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode

nl-fr.svg: Frisian flag, P.H. Wagemakers and Joh. Koopmans; SVG by Magna Frisia,
28 May 2026, released into the public domain by its author. Unchanged SVG.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Frisian_flag.svg

gb-con.svg, de-ofr.svg, de-nfr.svg: original MARS SVG encodings of traditional
simple geometric flags (St Piran, East Frisia black/red/blue, North Frisia
yellow/red/blue). The encodings are under the repository MIT Licence.
Design references:
https://www.flaginstitute.org/wp/flags/cornwall-flag/
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_East_Frisia.svg
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nordfriesischeflagge.svg

Catalan and Basque artwork reuses es-ct.svg and es-pv.svg from flag-icons 7.3.2,
under the adjacent MIT LICENSE. Normalised display proportions and animation
are applied at runtime; source SVGs are retained without modification.
Mongolia's Kazakh cultural display reuses kz.svg from the same flag-icons release,
paired with mn.svg. Both retain the adjacent MIT LICENSE; no new flag artwork is copied.

pl-kas.svg: original MARS geometric SVG encoding of the traditional Kashubian
black-over-yellow bicolour, under the repository MIT Licence.
Design reference: Kaszëbskô Jednota, "Herb, godło i flaga narodowa".
https://kaszebsko.com/herb-godlo-i-flaga-narodowa
