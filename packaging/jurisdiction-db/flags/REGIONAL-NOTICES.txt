gb-con.svg, de-ofr.svg, de-nfr.svg: original MARS SVG encodings of traditional
simple geometric flags (St Piran, East Frisia black/red/blue, North Frisia
yellow/red/blue). The encodings are under the repository MIT Licence.
Design references:
https://www.flaginstitute.org/wp/flags/cornwall-flag/
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_East_Frisia.svg
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nordfriesischeflagge.svg

Catalan and Basque artwork reuses es-ct.svg and es-pv.svg from flag-icons 7.3.2,
under the adjacent MIT LICENSE. Normalised display proportions and animation
are applied at runtime; source SVGs are retained without modification.
